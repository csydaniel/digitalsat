Passage
The chemist's early notebooks reveal a method that was, for its time, remarkably ___    : rather than isolating a single compound, she recorded every impurity alongside it, treating contamination as data rather than error.
Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?
Aconventional
Bmeticulous
Csystematic
Dcautious
Incorrect You chose B · Answer is C
"Meticulous" only describes care, not method — it doesn't capture that impurities were treated as data within a deliberate framework. "Systematic" is the only choice that names an approach, matching "method" earlier in the sentence.
